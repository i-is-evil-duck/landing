our fluffy values

The little things that make this community feel like home

  • 🌸
    Be Kind Treat everyone with respect and warmth. Bullying has no home here, ever.
  • ✨
    Be Yourself Express your truest self. We celebrate every shape, size, and style of being.
  • 🎨
    Be Creative Share your art, writing, music, crafts, or whatever you love making.
  • 🤝
    Be Supportive Lift each other up. Celebrate wins, comfort each other through tough times.
  • 🌈
    Be Inclusive Everyone belongs here. Racism, sexism, bigotry — simply not welcome.
  • 💫
    Be Safe Keep things SFW, tag spoilers, respect boundaries. Look out for one another.
